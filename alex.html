Research

Summer Undergraduate Researcher — Boies Lab

JUNE – SEPT 2026

STANFORD SCHOOL OF ENGINEERING

The Boies Lab makes carbon nanotubes in a floating-catalyst CVD reactor: gas flows through a tube inside a 1300 °C furnace, and the nanotubes form in the gas stream. My question was whether injecting against that flow (counterflow) instead of with it (main-flow) changes how much the reactor produces, and where along the tube the injector should sit.

WHAT I DID
Ran repeated experiments with a PhD collaborator, comparing counterflow and main-flow injection and moving the injection point along the tube.
RESULT
Counterflow injection raised CNT production to 216 mg/hr. The best injection point was 28.3 cm.
HARDWARE
Designed the quartz-tube flange assemblies in Fusion 360, including sealing geometry and injector-tube integration for high-temperature service. The design builds on the lab's earlier Flange v2.

Custom Aluminum Piano

FALL 2026 · ME 103 · IN PROGRESS
PROBLEM
Make a 20-key MIDI keyboard (E to B, 12 white and 8 black keys) entirely from 356A aluminum, using the PRL foundry and a manual mill, on a $130 budget.
WHAT I DID
Designed the frame, base, and keys for sand casting from 3D-printed PLA patterns (.060 in stock, 2° draft, ×1.013 shrink). The keys are cast as two bars, milled together, then sawed apart, so every key gets the same profile. All 20 pivot holes are drilled in one setup to line up on a Ø.250 in stainless rod. Wrote the operations sequence and the BOM.
RESULT
Not cast yet. The BOM comes to $104.84 against the $130 budget; electronics are a later phase.
WHAT I'D CHANGE
Writing the operations sequence caught five problems in CAD, fixed before printing patterns:
  1. The enclosed glass slot can't be sand cast. It became an open rabbet.
  2. The top skin was .044–.056 in, too thin for the casting to fill. It goes to about 3/16 in.
  3. Both pivot holes were blind, so the rod couldn't go in. The right one now goes through.
  4. The key stack had no end clearance. It gets about .010 in so the keys don't bind.
  5. The 4-40 screws reached only about .10 in into the frame. They go to 5/16 in long.

Spring-Powered Ping Pong Launcher

WINTER 2026 · ME 102
PROBLEM
Fire ping pong balls through a target board 5–7 ft away for two minutes with a purely mechanical spring launcher inside a 12 × 12 × 24 in envelope. Scored on how little static counterweight it needed to resist launch reaction forces.
WHAT I DID
Cut the counterweight through geometry instead of added mass. Backed it with free-body diagrams, force and moment balances, and stored-energy calculations, plus a dimensioned drawing package (tolerances, BOM, exploded views). Parts were laser-cut and 3D-printed.
RESULT
It fired through the target board at 5–7 ft over the two-minute run.
