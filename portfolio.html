Boies Lab

SUMMER UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCHER
JUNE – SEPT 2026
STANFORD SCHOOL OF ENGINEERING

The Boies Lab makes carbon nanotubes in a floating-catalyst CVD reactor: gas flows through a tube inside a 1300 °C furnace, and the nanotubes form in the gas stream. My question was whether injecting against that flow (counterflow) instead of with it (main-flow) changes how much the reactor produces, and where along the tube the injector should sit.

WHAT I DID
Ran repeated experiments with a PhD collaborator, comparing counterflow and main-flow injection and moving the injection point along the tube.
RESULT
Counterflow injection raised CNT production to 216 mg/hr. The best injection point was 28.3 cm.
HARDWARE
Designed the quartz-tube flange assemblies in Fusion 360: sealing geometry and injector-tube integration for high-temperature service. The design builds on the lab's earlier Flange v2.

Custom Aluminum Piano

ME 103 · FALL 2026 · IN PROGRESS

PROBLEM
Make a 20-key MIDI keyboard (12 white, 8 black keys) entirely from 356A aluminum, with the PRL foundry and a manual mill, on a $130 budget.
WHAT I DID
Designed the frame, base, and keys for sand casting from 3D-printed PLA patterns (.060 in stock, 2° draft, ×1.013 shrink). The keys are cast as two bars, milled together, then sawed apart, so every key gets the same profile. All 20 pivot holes are drilled in one setup. Wrote the operations sequence and BOM.
RESULT
Not cast yet. BOM: $104.84 against the $130 budget; electronics are a later phase.
WHAT I'D CHANGE
Five problems caught in CAD before printing patterns:
  1. Enclosed glass slot can't be sand cast: now an open rabbet.
  2. Top skin .044–.056 in, too thin to fill: now about 3/16 in.
  3. Both pivot holes blind, so the rod couldn't go in: the right one now goes through.
  4. No end clearance on the key stack: about .010 in added.
  5. 4-40 screws reached only about .10 in: now 5/16 in long.

Spring-Powered Ping Pong Launcher

ME 102 PRODUCT REALIZATION · WINTER 2026

PROBLEM
Fire ping pong balls through a target board 5–7 ft away for two minutes with a purely mechanical spring launcher inside a 12 × 12 × 24 in envelope. Scored on how little static counterweight it needed.
WHAT I DID
Cut the counterweight through geometry instead of added mass. Backed it with free-body diagrams, force and moment balances, stored-energy calculations, and a dimensioned drawing package (tolerances, BOM, exploded views). Parts were laser-cut and 3D-printed.
RESULT
It fired through the target board at 5–7 ft over the two-minute run.

Outdoor Stage Build

SIGMA NU SUNSPLASH · 2026 · ~1,340 RSVPS

PROBLEM
A stage, DJ table, and tiered platforms on sand for an outdoor event with ~1,340 RSVPs, built from lumber.
WHAT I DID
Modeled the layout in Fusion 360: 17 platforms in four sizes, 8 fence sections, and the DJ table. Sized the 2×4, 4×4, and plywood members from the model; then we bought the lumber and built it on site. The aluminum truss was bought, not built.
RESULT
Built on site and used for the event.
