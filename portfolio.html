Custom Aluminum Piano

ME 103 · FALL 2026 · IN PROGRESS

A 20-key MIDI keyboard, E to B (12 white keys, 8 black), made entirely of 356A aluminum. The frame, base, and key blanks are sand cast in the PRL foundry from 3D-printed PLA patterns, then finished on a manual mill. The keys pivot on a Ø.250 in stainless rod, and a glass window sits in the back wall.

All 12 white keys are cast and machined as one 11 in bar, and the 8 black keys as one 4.2 in bar, so every key gets the same profile. The bars are sawed apart afterward. All 20 pivot holes are drilled in one setup so they line up on the rod.

Writing the operations sequence turned up five things to fix in CAD before printing patterns. The enclosed glass slot can't be sand cast, so it became an open rabbet. The top skin was .044–.056 in thick, too thin for the casting to fill. Both pivot holes were blind, so the rod couldn't go in. The key stack had no end clearance, and the screws reached only about .10 in into the frame.

The BOM comes to $104.84 against a $130 budget. Electronics are a later phase.

Spring-Powered Ping Pong Launcher

ME 102 PRODUCT REALIZATION · WINTER 2026

A purely mechanical, spring-driven launcher inside a 12 × 12 × 24 in envelope. It fired through a target board at 5–7 ft over a two-minute run.

The scored design metric was the static counterweight resisting launch reaction forces. I cut it with geometry instead of added mass.

The paper trail: free-body diagrams, force and moment balances, stored-energy calculations, and a dimensioned drawing package with tolerances, BOM, and exploded views. Parts were laser-cut and 3D-printed. The Fusion assembly has six parts: base, barrel, funnel, slider, slot, and shaft.

Outdoor Stage Build

SIGMA NU SUNSPLASH · 2026 · ~1,340 RSVPS

A wooden stage, DJ table, and tiered platforms for Sigma Nu's Sunsplash. I modeled it in Fusion 360: 17 platforms in four sizes (a 2 ft stage, 1 ft and standard die tables, and 4 ft die tables with rails), 8 fence sections, and the DJ table.

I sized the 2×4, 4×4, and plywood members from that model. Then we bought the lumber and built it on site.

The aluminum truss over the DJ table was bought, not built. I DJ'd the party from the table.
